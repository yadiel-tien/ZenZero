PREFACE

These documents form six chapters organised around “one AlphaZero loop”: Chapter 1 is the getting-started manual, the other five explain the design. The technical chapters follow a paper-like house style: abstract, numbered sections, a notation table, captioned figures, numbered equations; every number states its measurement conditions, and negative results and post-mortems are written in as honestly as the wins. Suggested reading order: §1 How to Use (run it first) → §6 Architecture → §2 → §3 → §4 → §5 (then go deep); every chapter is self-contained, so skimming like a reference book is fine. Formula symbols are defined up front in each chapter's notation section; cross-chapter references look like “§4 ch. eq. (1)”.
